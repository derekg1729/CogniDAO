# CogniDAO Community Member Agreement

This Community Member Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into by and between CogniDAO and the undersigned individual or entity (“Member”). If the signer acts on behalf of an entity, the signer represents authority to bind that entity.

---

## 1. Definitions

**Community Network** means CogniDAO, its sub‑DAOs, working groups, and verified member organizations listed in the registry identified in Annex A.
**Competitive Offering** means any product or service offered to third parties that is marketed or functions as a substitute for, or that materially competes with, the Projects or with CogniDAO products or services that materially rely on the Projects.
**Contributions** means code, documentation, or other materials submitted by Member to the Projects.
**Member Services** means applications or services operated by Member solely for Community Network users under this Agreement.
**Projects** means the repositories identified in Annex A.
**Public License** means the license text located in each repository’s `LICENSE` file.

## 2. Relationship to Public License

2.1 The Projects remain publicly licensed under the applicable Public License.
2.2 This Agreement grants Member additional rights as set out in Section 5. These rights apply only while Member is in good standing and only for Member Services.
2.3 As between CogniDAO and Member, this Agreement governs in the event of conflict. Rights of non‑members under the Public License are unaffected.

## 3. Membership

3.1 Membership begins when the onboarding steps designated by CogniDAO are completed and Member appears in the registry in Annex A.
3.2 Member must keep registry information accurate, comply with the Code of Conduct and Acceptable Use in Annex B, and meet any published participation requirements.
3.3 CogniDAO may suspend or revoke membership for material breach in accordance with Section 12.

## 4. Inbound Contribution License (Harmony‑based)

4.1 Ownership. Member retains ownership of Contributions. This Agreement is a license, not an assignment.
4.2 Copyright License. Member grants CogniDAO a perpetual, worldwide, non‑exclusive, royalty‑free license to use, reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, and distribute Contributions and derivative works, with the right to relicense under the Projects’ outbound terms.
4.3 Patent License. Member grants CogniDAO and downstream recipients a perpetual, worldwide, non‑exclusive, royalty‑free patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer Contributions, covering patent claims that are necessarily infringed by the Contributions alone or by combination with the unmodified Project. This license terminates if Member initiates patent litigation alleging that the Project or a Contribution infringes a patent.
4.4 Moral Rights. To the extent permitted by law, Member waives, or agrees not to assert, moral rights in Contributions against CogniDAO.
4.5 Origin. Member represents that each Contribution is original or submitted with sufficient rights.

## 5. Outbound Member License

Subject to Sections 6 through 11 and while a Member in good standing, CogniDAO grants Member a non‑exclusive, worldwide, non‑transferable, non‑sublicensable license to:
(a) operate Member Services by hosting and running the Projects for Community Network users;
(b) create, use, and deploy derivative works of the Projects to provide Member Services;
(c) distribute the Projects and derivatives within the Community Network for Member Services; and
(d) distribute client software to end users of Member Services as reasonably necessary to access such services, subject to applicable Public Licenses for those components.

## 6. Restrictions

6.1 No Third‑Party Resale. Member may not offer, license, sell, or provide the Projects or derivatives to non‑members as a product or service, and may not operate them as a managed service for non‑members.
6.2 No Competitive Offering. Member may not provide a Competitive Offering outside the Community Network.
6.3 No Sublicensing. Member may not grant licenses to non‑members.
6.4 Public License Controls Outside Community Use. For all other uses, Member’s rights are limited to those granted by the Public License.

## 7. Reciprocity for Deployments

If Member operates Member Services using modified versions of the Projects, Member will submit the modifications to CogniDAO under Section 4 within a commercially reasonable time after production deployment to Community Network users.

## 8. Trademarks

No trademark license is granted. Use of CogniDAO names and marks is governed by the trademark policy referenced in Annex A and applicable law. Member must preserve license and attribution notices.

## 9. Confidentiality

Projects are developed in public. Member will not submit confidential information. Operational secrets such as keys or credentials are governed by separate operational policies, if any.

## 10. Compliance

Member must comply with the Code of Conduct and Acceptable Use in Annex B. Breach of those policies is a material breach of this Agreement.

## 11. Term; Suspension; Termination; Cure

11.1 Term. This Agreement is effective on acceptance and continues while Member remains a Member.
11.2 Suspension. CogniDAO may suspend Member rights immediately for violations affecting security, privacy, or safety.
11.3 Notice and Cure. For other breaches, either party may give written notice. If curable, the breaching party has thirty (30) days to cure after notice. A second material breach of the same kind within twelve (12) months may be terminated immediately.
11.4 Effect of Termination. On termination or revocation: (a) rights under Section 5 end; (b) rights under the Public License remain; (c) Sections 4, 8, 9, 10, 11.4, and 12 survive.

## 12. Warranty; Liability

THE PROJECTS ARE PROVIDED “AS IS.” TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. COGNIDAO’S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED USD $100.

## 13. General

13.1 Governing Law and Venue are specified in Annex A.
13.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject and supersedes prior discussions.
13.3 Amendments. Amendments must be in writing and posted in repository governance documents.
13.4 Assignment. Member may not assign this Agreement without CogniDAO’s written consent, except to a successor to all or substantially all relevant business that agrees in writing to be bound.
13.5 Independent Contractors. The parties are independent contractors.
